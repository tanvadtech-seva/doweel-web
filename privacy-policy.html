1. Information We Collect

DoWheels is designed to provide its functionality without requiring users to create a traditional account or provide personal information such as their name, phone number, physical address, or profile information.

We do not intentionally collect or store the following information through DoWheels:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Physical address
  • Profile photo
  • Government identification documents
  • Payment or banking information
  • Location information
  • Advertising identifiers for advertising purposes

The App does not use analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, or similar tracking services.

2. Google Sign-In

DoWheels provides Google Sign-In as an optional authentication method.

If you choose to use Google Sign-In, Google may provide the App with information necessary to authenticate you, such as an authentication identifier and basic account information.

Important: Google Sign-In is optional. You are not required to use Google Sign-In in order to use features of the App that do not require authentication.

Google processes information according to its own privacy policies and terms. We recommend reviewing Google's privacy practices for more information about how Google handles information associated with your Google Account.

DoWheels does not use Google Sign-In information for advertising, profiling, or selling personal information.

3. Camera and Photos

DoWheels may request access to your device's camera and photo library when you choose to capture or select service-related documents.

Camera and photo access is used only to allow you to capture or select documents required for the App's functionality.

You control whether the App is granted camera or photo access through your device's permission settings.

If you do not grant camera or photo permissions, you may still use other parts of the App that do not require those permissions.

4. Local Storage

Service documents captured or selected through DoWheels are stored locally on your device.

These locally stored documents are not intentionally uploaded by DoWheels to Tanva Tech's servers.

The availability and security of locally stored files may also depend on the security settings, operating system, and storage protections of your device.

If you uninstall the App or remove locally stored files using your device's controls, locally stored App data may no longer be available.

5. Information Sharing

Tanva Tech does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

We do not intentionally share your personal information with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party marketing companies.

Information processed by third-party services, such as Google when you voluntarily use Google Sign-In, is handled according to those providers' respective privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services

DoWheels may use Google Sign-In to provide optional authentication.

Apart from services required for optional Google authentication, DoWheels does not currently use third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, payment, mapping, or notification services.

Third-party services may have their own terms and privacy policies. We encourage users to review the applicable privacy policies of third-party providers they choose to use.

7. Children's Privacy

DoWheels is intended for general use and is not specifically directed toward children.

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children or require users to provide personal information to create an account.

Parents or guardians who believe that a child has provided personal information to us may contact us using the information provided below.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through DoWheels.

Because service documents may be stored locally on your device, you are also responsible for maintaining appropriate security on your device, including device passwords, screen locks, operating system security updates, and access controls.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, while we take reasonable precautions, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

DoWheels does not maintain a user account database containing personal profile information based on the current functionality of the App.

Service documents captured or selected through the App may remain on your device until you remove them or they are otherwise deleted according to your device's storage management.

You are responsible for managing locally stored documents on your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable legal requirements.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date displayed at the top of this Privacy Policy.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how DoWheels handles information.
